# Energy Storage Course Activities

An interactive, web-based in-class team design activity for **GSOE9111 / ENGG4111 Energy Storage** at **UNSW Sydney**.

---

## 🔋 Overview

In this activity, students form small engineering consultancy teams to solve real-world energy storage challenges. For each scenario, teams analyze a client brief specifying priorities across key performance dimensions:
- **Specific Energy** (Wh/kg)
- **Cost Index** ($)
- **Operational Safety** (thermal runaway resistance, dendrite risks, toxicity)
- **Power Capability** (discharge rate capability)
- **Cycle & Shelf Lifespan** (reversibility and secondary cycling stability)

Teams select candidate **anodes**, **cathodes**, and **electrolytes**, immediately observing the theoretical thermodynamic cell potential, capacity, and electrochemical compatibility.

---

## ⚡ Key Educational Features

- **Real-Time Physics Engine**: Instant calculation of cell voltage (E°_cell = E°_cathode − E°_anode), harmonic active material capacity, and theoretical specific energy.
- **Electrochemical Constraint Checking**: Warns against water-splitting overvoltage (aqueous electrolyte breakdown window), dendrite short-circuit risks on metallic recharge, cathode dissolution, and non-rechargeable chemistry limitations.
- **Dynamic Cell Schematic**: Generates an interactive schematic diagram showing electrode polarities, electrolyte bath ions, external load, and electron flow direction.
- **Live Classroom Collaboration**: Synchronized rounds with countdown timers, team locking mechanisms, and live performance leaderboards.

---

## 👥 How to Participate

1. Open the course activity link provided by the course lecturer.
2. Enter the **4-letter session code** displayed on the lecture hall screen (or scan the on-screen QR code).
3. Enter your team / consultancy name and click **Join**.
4. When the lecturer launches a round, review the client requirements and engineer your optimal cell chemistry!
